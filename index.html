About Us

More than a grassroots initiative.

Wasanii Sanaa operates as a self-organized group empowering marginalized populations through the dynamic power of performing arts and active community involvement.

Our Mission

To empower youth and marginalized communities to drive Social Justice, Human rights, and sustainable change through innovative, art-based education and advocacy.

Our Vision

A vibrant, Equitable society where every youth voice shapes a just future through the transformative power of art.

Our Profile & Background

Headquartered in the heart of Kibera informal settlement, Wasanii Sanaa uses edutainment—a fusion of education and entertainment—as a tool for advocacy and capacity-building. Our work emphasizes Theatre for Development, which involves interactive performances that spark rich community dialogues on pressing local matters.

As a learning organization, Wasanii Sanaa is consistently focused on learning, reflecting, and incorporating innovative design ideas across all its social projects. We truly embody the potential of the youth to lead, change, and make a massive difference.

Key Achievements

  • ✔ Participated in the 2025 XVIII Festival Sztuki Ludowej in Poland and Sanio Mondial Festivals in Italy.
  • ✔ Created awareness on peaceful coexistence in different communities with Life & Peace Institute and Mercy Corps Kenya.
  • ✔ Empowered communities on the reduction of GBV alongside Gender Violence Recovery Centre and Women Empowerment Link.

What We Do

Core Action Hubs.

We utilize targeted participatory frameworks to advocate for sustainable socio-economic growth.

1. Peace Building Initiative

Using performing art to resolve deep-seated community conflicts and actively promote sustainable unity. Our work includes structured community performances and workshops that encourage safe dialogue across tribal and clan lines, directly contributing to peace networks inside the urban areas.

2. Human Rights Education

Collaborating with global partners to tackle heavy grassroots operational issues such as structural governance, systemic poverty, drug addiction, early child marriages, police brutality, and gender-based violence (GBV). We ensure discussions remain thoroughly interactive rather than typical lectures.

3. Mentorship Frameworks

Tailored programs for adolescent students within schools and neighborhoods. Encompassing both boys and girls, we emphasize personal retention rates, boosting academic baseline outputs, and equipping teenagers with life skills to structurally decrease early pregnancies.

4. Health Interventions (SRHR & MHR)

Comprehensive health sensitization addressing Sexual Reproductive Health Rights and Maternal Health Rights. We work to lower unintended youth pregnancies and completely eliminate systemic myths or misconceptions via interactive forums, radio talk shows, and field events.

5. Theatre for Development (TfD)

Utilizing Participatory Educative Theatre or Forum Theatre structures to put operational control back into community hands. Performances directly emphasize regional cultural pride and establish spaces for modern youth to showcase organic artistry.
