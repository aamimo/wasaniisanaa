01 — Who we are

Born in Kibera, built for change.

Vision

A just, peaceful and empowered society where every voice — especially the marginalized — is heard through art.

Mission

To use participatory and forum theatre as edutainment for advocacy, dialogue and capacity-building across Kenya.

Motto

Unity gives strength. Art for change, voice for all.

02 — What we do

Five pillars. One stage.

01

03 — Milestones

A decade of stages, streets and schools.

  1. 2013

    Founded in Kibera

    A small group of young artists takes the stage to confront post-election tensions.
